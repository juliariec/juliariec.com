## Review

This novel suffered from an overabundance of ideas (and pages), and lacked the style that I loved in “The Inheritance of Loss”. I did enjoy Sonia's subplot with her lost amulet; Sunny's reflections on otherness and his experience dating an American; the mother-son dynamic between Sunny and Babita; and Mama's break with Papa and her move to Cloud Cottage.

_Maybe to drown out these questions about the future, they decided to swim one last time. Two people, united by the ocean-maybe nothing can undo that, the future cannot undo the past._
