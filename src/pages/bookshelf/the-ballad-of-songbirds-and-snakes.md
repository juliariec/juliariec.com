## Review

I read this book after enjoying the movie, and I enjoyed it but ultimately preferred the movie. Tom Blyth's portrayal of Coriolanus had more depth and ambiguity than he had in the novel, which made the story inherently more interesting._"And who wouldn't rather be the victor than the defeated?" "I don't know that I have much interest in being either," said Lysistrata. "But that wasn't an option," Coriolanus reminded her, "given the question. Not if you think about it."_
