## Review

I didn't find this book especially entertaining, but I admired the author's attention to detail and enjoyed reading about how her relationship with the hare developed over time. Judging from her research and the excerpts throughout the book, this memoir also seemed like a valuable contribution to literature about hares.

_I wondered how much of the nature of animals is obscured from us simply because of the limitations of our senses and powers of observation._
