## Review

I liked the humour in this, which was so aggressively Millennial that it was charming. As for the romance, I found Henry dull, and it felt far too 'established relationship' for my taste.

_Why doesn't this office keep normal milk around? This is why people from Texas hate Washington elites. Ruining the goddamn dairy industry._
