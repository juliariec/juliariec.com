## Review

I found this novel so beautiful and moving, and I loved the magic realism.

_In the end that he had imagined, they would both be old, as they had promised each other, and by then, they would have had time to say everything to one another and to say it many times, without caring that they repeated it, without ever growing tired of it. He had thought there would be time._
