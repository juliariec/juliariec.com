## Review

It's very hard to take people who have 'enemies' at work seriously, and this book suffered so much from a lack of plot that I had to read like 50 pages about a company paintball game.

_If he were here, he'd look at me, and when our eyes connected a flashbulb of energy would pop inside me. I'd label it annoyance, or dislike. I'd take the little flash and call it something I don’t think it is._
