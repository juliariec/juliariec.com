## Review

Something about this book didn't quite click for me: the pacing seemed odd, Toby's unreliability made it challenging to discern what was actually happening, and I didn't find the ending believable.

\_"The thing is, I suppose," he said, "that one gets into the habit of being oneself. It takes some great upheaval to crack that shell and force us to discover what else might be underneath."\*
